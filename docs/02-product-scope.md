# Corneer product scope

## Product promise

Corneer is intended to make the first serious B2B sourcing conversation easier:

> Understand who a company is, what it can likely produce, and what evidence was
> actually checked before choosing to make contact.

The POC uses custom sportswear and activewear as its initial demonstration
vertical. Hong Kong and Mainland China are the fictional supplier markets;
buyers are represented as international apparel brands and distributors.

## Users

### Buyers

Business owners, sourcing managers, procurement staff, and brand operators who
need to discover a supplier or publish a structured sourcing request.

### Suppliers

Manufacturers, factories, and transparent trading companies that want access
to relevant buyer demand. A trading company must not be presented as a
manufacturer.

### Administrators

Corneer operators who review submitted evidence, record limited verification
decisions, moderate RFQs, and respond to reports.

## Core objects

| Object             | Role in the product                                  |
| ------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| Company            | Primary trust and discovery object                   |
| Product showcase   | Representative capability that links to a company    |
| RFQ                | Structured and time-bounded buyer requirement        |
| Supplier response  | Fit statement and indicative commercial terms        |
| Conversation       | Communication after the parties show mutual interest |
| Verification check | One transparent fact, method, date, and result       |

## RFQ qualification

A useful apparel RFQ should provide enough information for an eligible supplier
to decide whether to respond:

- Product category and concise requirement.
- Estimated quantity and style or color split.
- Material or fabric needs.
- Required production capabilities.
- Delivery market and target date.
- Response deadline.
- Certification or documentation requirements.
- Tech-pack or sample status.

The buyer's legal identity may remain private from suppliers, but Corneer must
know and check the buyer before distributing the request.

## Verification language

Public language must answer four questions:

1. What fact was checked?
2. What evidence or method was used?
3. When was it checked?
4. Which related claims remain self-reported?

Acceptable examples include:

- “Business registration checked.”
- “Representative identity checked.”
- “Factory video reviewed.”
- “Production capacity supplier-reported.”

Do not use “fully verified,” “guaranteed supplier,” or equivalent blanket
assurances without a defensible, documented audit scope.

## POC success condition

The current frontend succeeds when a reviewer can understand the complete
journey in a few minutes and give concrete feedback about missing information.
It does not validate marketplace demand.

The first operational pilot should treat a shortlisted supplier and scheduled
buyer meeting as the initial matching success event. A quotation, sample
request, negotiation, or purchase order is stronger commercial validation.

## Outside the POC

- Real buyer or supplier onboarding.
- Legal or capability verification operations.
- Persistent RFQs, responses, conversations, or files.
- Email and meeting-provider integrations.
- Payments, escrow, disputes, logistics, and customs.
- Public supplier reviews.
- Subscription billing and paid ranking.
- Automated matching and recommendation models.
